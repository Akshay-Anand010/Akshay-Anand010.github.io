Akshay is a Senior Software Engineer at Visa in Bengaluru (November 2025 to present). The domain is security and identity: OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, authentication, authorization, and access control. His contributions there include functional and non-functional analysis, scalability and performance engineering, and technical documentation.

Before Visa he was a Developer at Thoughtworks in Bengaluru (April 2024 to October 2025), on the Purchase Availability team for the John Deere account. He built scalable e-commerce services for global cart, product availability, and promotions. That included a multi-brand universal cart, a promotion engine using Voucherify, the Save For Later feature, Kafka consumers for domain events, and caching that cut database load by about 40 percent. The stack there included Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, Azure API Management, Grafana, and Datadog.

Before Thoughtworks he was a Software Engineer at Manhattan Associates in Bengaluru (September 2021 to April 2024), on the Manhattan Active Omni order management system. He worked on order creation, allocation, fulfillment, and returns, and integrated PayPal, Cybersource, and Aurus for auth, capture, refund, and reversal. The stack included Java, Spring Boot, microservices, REST, and SQL.

He was a Software Engineer intern at Infoblox from January 2021 to June 2021, on the IT team, working with Salesforce CRM, a receipt-generation document repository, and an Apex email alert. He was a web development intern at Kavach Networks from May 2020 to August 2020, and a web development intern at Exposys Data Labs from April 2020 to May 2020.

Across the career he has used Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, RabbitMQ, GCP, AWS, Docker, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, Grafana, and Datadog. He is strong on high-level and low-level design, domain-driven design, and system design for scale. He uses Claude and Cline day to day for design, debugging, and code review.

Honors on his profile include Smart India Hackathon 2020 finalist, an IEEE hackathon win, and mobile control robotics.
